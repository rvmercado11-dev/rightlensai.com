Perspective

A system can go live without the organization becoming better.

Technology creates lasting value only when the work, information, decisions, safeguards and human responsibilities around it operate together. Before buying or expanding AI, begin with the problem and the result that matters.

The RightLens AI Value TestAI Value = Better Results + Right Safeguards + People in Control

This is not arithmetic. All three must be present, and the test applies to the complete workflow, not only to the AI tool.

Discuss the Discovery Sprint

What the Sprint helps clarify

1. What really needs to improve?

Examine the business problem, desired outcome and current way of working.

2. Does AI genuinely belong?

Determine whether AI is appropriate and what practical improvement it could produce.

3. What responsibilities and boundaries are needed?

Clarify who is accountable, what information the AI may use, where human review is required and how the change may affect people and costs.

4. What evidence should guide the next step?

Define what a bounded pilot should measure before the organization expands the use of AI.

What you receive

An AI Decision Brief and prioritized 90-day roadmap for the agreed next step. Together, these cover the use case, expected value, accountable ownership, data and human review boundaries, key risks and evidence required for the next decision.

Intended result

A better-supported decision to proceed, address specific conditions, redesign the use case, defer it or not proceed.

Scope boundary: The Sprint is a leadership decision and readiness diagnostic, not AI implementation or a legal or technical audit. Where specialist work is needed, the Sprint will identify it as part of the recommended next step.
